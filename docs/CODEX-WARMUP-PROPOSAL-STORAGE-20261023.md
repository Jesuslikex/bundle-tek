Cheap owned namespace/legacy/quota preflights avoid replacement provider work for known refusals, and final SQL repeats all race-sensitive constraints. A permanently abandoned save needs a new original review and new IDs. Lost-response/cancellation recovery is saved-only; no write or provider retry occurs inside a failed call. Stage/root/consent/review identifiers are disjoint and new proposals cannot upgrade through either old consent table, in either insertion order. Staged results may carry reconstructed advisory content but future approval must require an independently re-read publication row; a caller-supplied status object is insufficient.
