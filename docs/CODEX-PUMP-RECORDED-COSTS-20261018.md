# Recorded Pump launch packet fees and tips

18 October 2026. Follow-up to local account history checkpoint `f3be26d`, on committed funding baseline `cea62f5`. This is a local read-only feature, not a release, funded acceptance result or complete Proxima parity.

## Behavior

Each recent account launch group now has a collapsed **Recorded fees and tip** detail. An explicit review loads its retained broadcast outcome, launch packet network fees, separate tip transaction fee and paid tip. Known fees include failed transactions. Exact integer arithmetic prevents rounding and prevents counting the closure's aggregate fee a second time.

Missing packet/tip evidence stays unknown. A partial statement is labelled a known subtotal; if no finalized cost amounts are available, the total and individual absent amounts are shown as unavailable/unknown rather than zero. An actually recorded failed tip transfer may correctly be zero. The displayed retained outcome makes a newer detail distinguishable from an older account-list state. Individual packet outcomes, original observation time and exclusions stay behind a second disclosure.

The action handles non-JSON errors, expired sign-in, explicit retry and a 15-second client deadline. Refresh clears older amounts first. Request identity, context binding, abort cleanup and cancelled deadlines prevent late replies from replacing a newer selection or restoring stale amounts. Buttons have a 44px minimum height; technical details and requests remain opt-in.

## Evidence and authority boundary

Authenticated, no-store `GET /api/account-launch-activity/costs?reviewId=<uuid>` accepts exactly one saved group selector. It derives the owner from the session; foreign and missing reviews share the same 404. Internal storage/provider details are withheld on failure.

The new server reader uses the existing real saved-review/signature/broadcast parsers. It loads the exact owner/review/group observation referenced by the retained broadcast recovery, binds its context, manifest, signed-group identity, source state and digest, and revalidates that primary observation. The projection reconciles each packet's fee, the verified tip outcome/amount and the closure's independent totals and references. It verifies the classified terminal outcome and partial counts, preserves missing coverage, and rechecks the owned saved group before publishing. Missing or inconsistent lineage withholds all totals.

It has no provider callback, does not collect new chain observations and issues SELECTs only. Existing group generation, consent, fresh code/custody checks, serial-read defaults, recovery transitions and reservation rules are unchanged. The response contains no signed bytes or signatures. Every economic-settlement, profit, purchase-principal, rent, signing, execution and reservation-change authority flag remains false.

This projection currently requires a retained **closed Pump group broadcast** record. Unsigned groups and other terminal/launch formats remain unavailable here; the UI directs the user to project recovery history. It is separate from the existing account swap-cost totals. It does not determine realized launch P&L, live balances/value, purchase principal, rent, external costs or complete economic settlement, and it does not prove Jito atomicity.
