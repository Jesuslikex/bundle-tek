# DCA end-time control, 13 October 2026

The DCA composer now offers an optional end time in the user's local timezone, previews its canonical UTC value, and saves it with immutable session terms. It must be strictly after the final scheduled cycle. A new plan must also end in the future, enforced again by the database clock during insertion. Existing sessions without a deadline retain their original canonical representation and behavior.

At the deadline, no new cycle preparation, approval or submission is permitted. `captureTradeSession` rejects ended terms before provider reads; the final session write guard checks the retained deadline against database time. Append-only migration 0075 extends preparation, approval and submission timing triggers with the same database deadline check. Creating deadline-bearing plans requires those guards to be installed. Old 75 SQL files and journal entries remain unchanged.

The session clock follows the deadline after the final due time, then marks cycles ended and disables fresh preparation, queued-job controls and any open fresh preparation panel. Queuing already-ended work is rejected by the model service. Pause/cancel and historical approval recovery remain available. Registering an already-created exact signature and recovering its receipt do not require a currently open DCA execution window; neither operation authorizes a new broadcast.
